When an AI agent, an app, or a service needs verified data about a user — or
permission to act on their behalf — it asks Permyt in plain English. The user
sees the request on their phone and taps Accept or Deny. If they approve,
Permyt issues scoped tokens that let the requester reach the source services
directly.

Permyt brokers the authorization. It never sees, stores, or handles the data
itself.

## What you can do with Permyt

A lender asks for the data it needs to assess a mortgage and the customer
approves it once — income from their bank, employment from an HR system, debts
from a credit bureau, identity from a passport authority. An agent reads
ninety days of transactions with a single tap instead of a shared password. A
recruiter confirms employment without chasing a letter. Every one of these is
the same shape: a request in plain English, a decision on the user's phone,
and data that flows straight from the source.

The user's experience is the one they already know from their phone: answer
once, or answer always. Revoke at any time. Every access is logged.
